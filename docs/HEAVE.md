CHEF will be able to lift EGG objects, carry them around, and throw them.

Pressing DOWN + ATTCK (B) will trigger CHEF's lift action. CHEF's animation during this action is two frames: arm straight forward in a grab, and arm up to lift over his head. CHEF will go through these frames whether he's successfully lifting something or not; a "miss" is still animated with no lifted object. If no object was lifted, CHEF returns to normal sprites after this action is complete

If CHEF is over an EGG when he lifts, the egg will become lifted and sit atop CHEF. EGG passes through obstacles so that CHEF could pass through a CHEF-sized opening without EGG blocking his way. If CHEF lifts an EGG, then Chef's sprite resumes action with its arm pointed straight up. Sprites will eed to exist for CHEF idle, in motion, and jumping with that arm up. CHEF can't swing his pan or grab ledges when he's carrying an EGG.

By prassing ATTACK (B) while carrying an EGG, CHEF will toss the egg. EGG travels about 5 tiles from a single toss. CHEF's toss animation is just the "grab" straight arm frame, then a return to normal/idle.

A tossed EGG takes one hit of damage:
- an uncracked egg will become cracked when it lands
- a cracked egg will despawn and reveal any treasure it may have contained when it lands

If an EGG strikes a MOB, it deals damage equivalent to TWO hits from a PAN.
