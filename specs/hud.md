# HUD

Status: **three-heart display and half-heart damage behavior implemented and
accepted**. Death and Game Over were implemented for review on 2026-10-14;
healing is not implemented.

## Health

- `CurrentHealth` stores zero through six half-heart units.
- A new run starts at six health: three filled hearts.
- Hearts drain from right to left. An odd value displays a filled left half and
  empty right half in the rightmost remaining heart.
- Level transitions preserve health. Returning to the title after Game Over and
  starting a new run restores all three hearts.

## Rendering

The HUD uses three fixed 8×8 sprites at screen X positions 112, 128, and 144.
Each sprite selects a full, half-full, or empty heart tile. The source art is
generated by `tools/generate-hud.mjs`; OBJ indices 73–75 hold the three states.

The HUD does not use world coordinates, so camera movement cannot displace it.
Three sprites are used instead of six half-heart sprites to limit OAM and
per-scanline pressure. During the frying-pan attack the game owns eleven OAM
entries in total, but the three HUD sprites occupy scanlines 0–7 while the
camera normally keeps the eight Chef/pan sprites near mid-screen. If an attack
overlaps the HUD at the extreme top of the world, gameplay sprites retain OAM
priority and a heart may briefly drop out.

The originally proposed Window-layer strip was rejected after SameBoy testing:
the DMG Window trigger remains active for the frame once drawing begins, and
mid-frame cutoff attempts produced either a covered playfield or a vertical
trigger seam. A robust top-edge Window HUD would require a more invasive raster
system than the current polling loop warrants.
